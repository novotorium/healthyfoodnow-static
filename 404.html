A fond farewell, for now

For years, Healthy Food Now offered coaching, recipes and guidance to people who wanted to eat better. Plant-based, nutrient-dense, one honest step at a time. Today we’re saying goodbye.

What we believe hasn’t changed. Eating well shouldn’t be this hard, and doing it alone is the hardest way.

We hope to be back, to rebuild this and the community around it better than before. We can’t say when.

Until then, the recipes are yours. Every one our chefs and coaches developed, free to read, search and print, with nothing to sign up for.

Thank you for cooking with us.
